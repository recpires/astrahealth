## What these results show — and what they do not

- They show that the prototype behaves as specified in the test plan for the three scripted scenarios: measurement, analysis, response guidance and delayed Earth communication, end to end.
- All vital-sign data is **simulated** (seeded, reproducible). No real astronaut data is involved.
- Thresholds, rules, escalation and checklists are **provisional demonstration choices**; passing these tests does **not** validate them clinically, and the source for analysis thresholds is still pending team confirmation.
- Mission times are deterministic (simulated clock). Real-time durations in the browser checks depend on the machine and are indicative only.

_Observed values are reported as produced by the application (English interface)._
